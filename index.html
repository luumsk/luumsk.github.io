Current projects

Optimization of multi-agent communication

Under reviewMulti-agent

Lets a team of LLM agents learn who should talk to whom, turning task feedback into edge-level textual gradients that reshape the communication graph.

Budgeted oversight for reliable AI agents

Under reviewOversight

Deciding which agent outputs to verify when checks are costly, so a limited oversight budget goes where it reduces the most risk.

Capability-aware reasoning in vision-language models

Under reviewVLM benchmark

A paired benchmark in which navigation questions differ in exactly one property of the agent, testing whether VLMs reason about who can actually traverse a route.

Representation learning for medical image analysis

OngoingMedical AI

Foundation models for brain MRI that handle any combination of imaging modalities, and lesion-aware training that recovers small structures standard models miss.
