About

I'm a second‑year PhD student in AI for medical imaging, focusing on brain MRI. My work combines data‑centric analysis, foundation‑model pretraining, and robust evaluation under missing or heterogeneous modalities.

I enjoy building practical, well‑engineered tools: reproducible preprocessing pipelines, clean training code, and small agentic systems that actually solve tasks.
